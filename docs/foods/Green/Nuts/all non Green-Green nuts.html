All non Green-Green nuts

All non Green-Green nuts is a Green food. Green foods are safe to eat as a regular part of a diet.

All tree nuts are acceptable sources of good protein, as well as products made from them

Around half the weight of a nut is oil, much of it omega-6 fatty acids. Nuts are therefore caloriedense and tend to upset the omega-3 to omega-6 balance. For these reasons, nuts should be consumed in moderation.

Paleo in a Nutshell — 3. How Did It Go Wrong?, p. 41

Monounsaturated fats were quite common in our ancestral environment, particularly in the marrow of bones and many nuts. Our bodies know how to handle them in a healthy way. Health benefits of monounsaturated fats include lowering cholesterol levels, reducing the risk of heart disease and stroke, and enhancing cell building and the immune system. Today, our most common source is olive oil. Avocado is another good source.
