Additional Quotes

Specific references to Ketchup, Regular throughout Geoff Bond's publications.

Deadly Harvest — 3. How We Eat and Its Consequences, p. 54

The U.S. Department of Agriculture (USDA) does not subdivide its Vegetable Group: they classify french fries and ketchup as vegetables just like lettuce and broccoli. As this example shows, it does indeed make a difference just what kind of vegetable we are eating— not all "vegetables" conform to the type of plant food to which we are naturally adapted.
